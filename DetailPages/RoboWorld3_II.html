STATEMENT

This is s a site-responsive 2D visual novel set in a fictional post-human machine society. Players interact with the laptop to enter the ROBOs’ world, while also exploring the surrounding installation to find fragments of its language, beliefs, and social systems.

In the 2D game section, players will randomly encounter several dialogues between ROBOs and have the power to influence the 'nighttime' story clips of the ROBOs. Through this interactive approach, players can get a glimpse of this robotic society.

《RW3：罗莫顿》是一款响应式2D视觉小说，背景设定在虚构的未来无人碳基生命社会中。玩家通过操作笔记本电脑进入ROBO们的世界，同时在场玩家可通过探索周边装置，搜寻其语言、信仰和社会系统的世界观碎片。

在电子游戏部分，玩家会随机接触到几段Robo之间的对话，且有权力影响Robo们‘夜晚’的故事发展。通过这种互动方式，玩家得以窥见这个机器社会的一角。
