Robo World II - Taste Machine

VIDEO_DOCUMENTATION // BILIBILI_EMBED
STATEMENT

Building on the fictional setting Robo World that I created in Dec. 2025, this time I approached the post-human, non-human world from a new angle:

Taste is a sense that machines can barely simulate. Yet the Robos, who carry forward human culture and habit, want to learn how to 'taste'. Using human scientific frameworks as their reference, they attempt to construct a machine capable of perceiving food. They search for food-related data in industrial products and human imagery in order to form a basic food model.

In this project, I made extensive use of AI. Firstly, I asked ChatGPT to study the existed electronic tongues and their data outputs. It then helped me generate the names and functions of five main components of the Taste Machine. After that, I used AI image-generation and AI modelling tools to create the initial models for these five parts. Once I had refined the models and materials, I placed the Taste Machine in my 3D digital game environment. Finally, I designed the interaction logic and animation in Unreal Engine so that the machine could be presented through player interaction. Taste Machine also offers an indirect way of presenting Robo World, which is a little society that continues the legacy of human intelligence and civilisation. In the physical exhibition space, I also displayed the 'journey' and posters to help audiences understand the project.

基于我在2023年12月创建的一格架空设定“Robo World”，我选用了一个新的角度来描绘这个深受后人类时代影响的无人的世界：

味觉是个机器几乎无法模拟也没必要去模拟的人类感官。而延续人类文明习惯的robo们希望学习如何“品尝”到食物。于是它们用人类的科学思维框架和参照，尝试去构建一个味觉感知器。又从工业加工品的数据和人类影像中搜寻食物信息，以此获得基础模型。然后他们仿照人类的电子舌技术和合理想象，发明创造了一台机器。当“食物”被投入进溶液，机器就会自动运作，生成数据报告并调出影像记录。

在本项目制作中，我大量地使用了AI。首先我让ChatGPT学习了电子舌技术和其的数据输出形式。然后它帮我列出了5个组成Taste Machine的主要部件的名称及功能。此后我使用AI绘图软件和AI建模软件生成了这五个部件的基本模型。在修改模型和材质后，我将这个Taste Machine放入了我的3D数字游戏空间。最后我在Unreal Engine中设计了交互逻辑和动画。通过玩家交互的方式，这个Machine被展出。通过这种方式，也是侧面描绘了这个延续人类智慧文明的Robo世界。我也在实体展览空间展出了‘日志’和海报，便于观众理解。

TECHNICAL TOOLS Blender, UE5, SD, PR
